# Raccoon Lift -- Scene 04: "The Big Idea" (written under the standing authorization)

**Target:** ~60 s, 16:9. Next morning by the tower and the stream. Captions as .srt.
**Lesson:** COUNTERWEIGHT -- tie two loads to one rope over a pulley: the heavier side goes down, the other goes up
(lesson panel). Callback: the trip hammer's cams turned round into up-and-down (Episode 1).
**Build:** the lift frame: two tall posts with a crossbeam and two pulleys, a rope over them, the food box hanging on
one end and a bucket on the other. Blueprint on the Page Card (hands free: shown, then pocketed).
**Safety:** a heavy thing on a rope: stand clear underneath.

Science check: an Atwood machine / counterweight: two masses on a rope over a pulley -- the heavier one descends;
water-balance lifts (e.g. old funiculars and the Saltburn and Lynton & Lynmouth cliff railways) use water as the
counterweight. Never stand under a suspended load.
