# Raccoon Lift -- Scene 05: "Test 1: Cabbage Launch" (written under the standing authorization)

**Target:** ~55 s, 16:9. Next morning at the lift. Captions as .srt.
**Lesson:** WATER RUNS DOWNHILL (the flume starts upstream, where the water is higher) -- and CONTROL: open a gate a
little at a time (too much, too fast = trouble). Lesson panel.
**Build:** a wooden flume on trestles from upstream down to the lift, ending in a spout with a sluice gate (a little
wooden door that slides up) above the bucket. The box is down and loaded with cabbages; the bucket hangs up top, empty.
**Safety:** stand clear of a load that is moving.

Science check: water flows downhill (gravity); flumes and mill races take water from upstream to keep it flowing;
a sluice gate controls the flow rate; a counterweight that is far heavier accelerates fast.
Safety: he stands clear while it moves; the cabbage comes out of the stream later (no food left for wildlife).
