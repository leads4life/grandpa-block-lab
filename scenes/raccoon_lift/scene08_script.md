# Raccoon Lift -- Scene 08: "The Brake" (written under the standing authorization)

**Target:** ~60 s, 16:9. Same day, afternoon. Captions as .srt.
**Lesson:** CONTROL THE DRAIN (a valve lets water out slowly: the box comes down gently -- a brake). SMOOTH METAL:
claws can't grip it (raccoon baffles).
**Build:** a drain valve with a handle on the barrel; shiny metal baffles (wide collars) round both posts.
**Test:** the EGG TEST -- an egg rides up and down on the box without breaking.

Science check: draining through a partly opened valve lowers flow (and the barrel's weight) slowly; smooth sheet-metal
baffles are a standard way to stop raccoons and squirrels climbing poles.
