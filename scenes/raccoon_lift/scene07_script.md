# Raccoon Lift -- Scene 07: "Calibration Montage" (written under the standing authorization)

**Target:** ~55 s, 16:9. Next day, a quick montage of tests. Captions as .srt.
**Lesson:** Word Card PULLEY ("a wheel with a groove for a rope"). MECHANICAL ADVANTAGE (lesson panel): a pulley that
moves with the load is a trade -- here the box goes twice as high as the barrel drops, but it needs twice the water.
**Build:** a moving pulley on the barrel (block and tackle); a fill line painted on the barrel.

| # | Picture | Words |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Morning. He holds up two fingers to the lens. | GRANDPA: "Two problems. The box only goes up as far as the barrel comes down. And raccoons can reach a metre." |
| 2 | Word Card PULLEY. He points at the wheels on the beam. | "These wheels are pulleys. A wheel with a groove, so a rope can run over it." |
| 3 | BUILD: a pulley block on the barrel, the rope's end tied to the beam (block and tackle). | "Now watch. Ready, chat? Blocks up!" |
| 4 | Title TEST 3: a little water -- nothing moves. He shrugs. | "Test three. Too little water... nothing." |
| 5 | Title TEST 4: too much -- the box shoots up; he winces (the gate saves it). | "Test four. Too much. Easy, easy!" |
| 6 | Title TEST 5: just right -- the barrel sinks slowly, the box glides all the way to the top, twice as far. | "Test five..." |
| 7 | Lesson panel MECHANICAL ADVANTAGE. He paints a line on the barrel: FILL TO HERE. | "The barrel drops a little; the box goes up twice as far. That's the trade: twice the height, twice the water." |
| 8 | Chat: TobiasBuilds "mechanical advantage!!". He beams. | "Mechanical advantage. Tobias knows." |
| 9 | HOOK: the box at the top, out of reach. He looks at the posts, worried. | "Up high. Out of reach. But going DOWN... it drops like a stone. We need a brake." |

Science check: a single movable pulley gives a 2:1 ratio; reversed (the moving pulley on the counterweight), the load
travels twice as far as the counterweight but needs twice the force; pulleys are wheels with grooved rims.
