# Raccoon Lift -- Scene 06: "Test 2: Stuck Halfway" (written under the standing authorization)

**Target:** ~60 s, 16:9. Straight on from Scene 05 (same morning). Captions as .srt.
**Lesson:** BALANCED -- equal weights on both sides don't move (water leaking out as fast as it comes in keeps the
weight the same). And MEASURE: a stone-weight scale says how heavy the load is, so you know how much water you need.
**Safety (the agreed beat):** he rides the lift about 1 m and gets stuck; then, sincerely: never ride a load lift.
**Build:** the bucket swapped for a barrel (more water, more lift) under a platform for the box; a stone-weight scale.

Science check: a counterweight lift stops where the two sides balance; inflow equal to outflow keeps a container's
weight constant; a balance scale compares weights. Safety: a lift built for loads is never for people (riding it here
lasts ~1 m and he comes straight down -- the lesson is said plainly).
