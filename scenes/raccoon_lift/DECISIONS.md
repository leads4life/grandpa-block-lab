# Raccoon Lift: decisions made under the standing authorization

| Scene | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 02 | The three raccoons are a mama and two kits | Honest (raccoons forage as families) and it gives the episode its heart without making them villains; sets up the 3 raccoons of Scene 09 |
| 02 | The test is the crate itself, no bait box | A bait box would be feeding them (correction 2); she is sent off before she eats |
| 02 | Sent off by a clap from far away | Hazing is the safe, advised way; he never chases or corners her |
| 02 | Mama comes from the woods; the kits peek from the vent | The vent (Scene 01, 22 x 10 cm) is kit-sized; a grown raccoon would not fit |
