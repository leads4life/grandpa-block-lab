# Raccoon Lift -- Scene 02: "Locks Don't Work" (written under the standing authorization)

**Target:** ~65 s, 16:9. Next morning, then the same evening (golden light). Captions as .srt.
**Lesson:** DEXTEROUS (Word Card: "good with your hands"). Raccoon hands: five long fingers that feel everything.
**Build:** a wooden lid on a hinge with an iron hook latch (the stone lid failed).
**Safety:** watch from a distance; a clap and a word from far away, never chase or corner; never feed.
**Heart:** the "bandit" is a mama raccoon feeding two kits (callback to Episode 1: "the scariest thing in the woods... was just hungry").

Science check: raccoon front paws have five long, very sensitive fingers (they "see" with their hands); raccoons open
latches, jars and doors; they are mostly active at dusk and night; a mother raises kits in a den (under buildings is common);
hazing (noise from a distance) is the advised way to send one off.
