## Choose what deserves attention

Estimated 0.0–23.7s

Three choices: turn, creep forward, or wait. This example will show how a planner chooses where to spend effort. A pedestrian is crossing and an oncoming car is approaching. Our car waits. The other choices can still be represented, but they don't all deserve endless exploration. Tesla's patent application describes one way to make that search selective. First, watch what happens when we add just two more decision stages.

Visual: Begin inside a built-out 3D junction at car height; show the crossing, opposing vehicle, and three possible actions. Reveal waiting as the illustrative choice while the actors remain visible.

Audio: A concise decision prompt; emphasize 'waits' as the traffic interaction becomes clear. Keep the chosen narrator near the preferred measured pace.

Reward: Waiting is a candidate decision linked to the actual crossing and oncoming traffic.

## Connect the decisions

If the situation changes, they may matter to the next decision. We still need a way to compare the choices that remain.

## Pay off the pruning idea

Estimated 127.9–157.3s

The patent describes comparing node scores with a threshold and removing lower-scoring nodes. This is pruning. The poor branch stops growing, while a more promising branch develops more detail. Look back at the road: the pedestrian and cyclist are still there. We have removed a candidate from the search, not a person from the world. That can avoid work on trajectories that would not be selected. The method describes a way to focus effort. It does not give us a measured percentage saving for this example.

Visual: Tie the threshold to a specific branch; stop and retract that branch while the retained one gains detail. Maintain the pedestrian and cyclist in the underlying junction.

Audio: A brief anticipatory pause before the branch stops, followed by the causal explanation. No fake crash or alarm sound.

Reward: Threshold pruning stops developing a poor candidate and directs expansion toward promising nodes.

## Separate the hardware claim

The useful check is to match a feature claim to an actual release for the actual vehicle.
