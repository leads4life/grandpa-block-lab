Inside Tesla’s planning patent application: how a search can organize possible interactions, score choices and stop expanding poor branches.

Original moving 3D models illustrate the method. The scenes, scores, timing and compute loads are schematic examples, not a reconstruction or benchmark of Tesla’s software. This application does not establish HW3/V14 feature parity or an 80% measured saving. Tesla describes FSD (Supervised) as requiring active driver supervision.
