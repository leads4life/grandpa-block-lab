## What the new ladder changes

The draft starts with the driving choice, delivers an early answer, then builds eight useful rewards: choose → understand growth → connect interactions → preserve the road user → compare consequences → prune → check the hardware claim → apply the distinction. Exact spoken prompts and payoffs are mapped to shots, supporting verified claims, visual actions and narrator directions. Every promise is paid.

A carrot must point to a specific observable payoff: “Watch what happens to the cyclist when this branch goes away.” The actor then remains visible while the branch stops developing. “Stay until the end for a shocking upgrade” is neither a verified claim nor an earned reward.

The visual redesign replaces repeated bare-floor diagram presentations with deeper staged scenes. Street scenes establish consequences; spatial futures develop from the actual driving situation; scoring needs a visible motion consequence; hardware has a technical workbench. Camera changes expose the cause and its result.

The audio plan uses the chosen narrator, concise questions, selective emphasis and short anticipatory pauses. It keeps the agreed natural pace, restrained mechanically justified effects, no music and no narration captions. Word-rate and loudness checks cannot certify an engaging performance.

## What is now built

- New projects are marked retention-required at intake.
- Exact narration-quote anchors, supporting claim IDs, paid promises, knowledge rewards, visual actions, audio directions and forward ladder links are checked.
- Planning heuristics flag late hooks, late first rewards, long value gaps, long unpaid questions and too many simultaneous open loops. Their defaults are editable editorial choices, not YouTube science.
- Production approval requires measured frozen narration plus a current editorial review bound to script, timing and sources. Changed content makes that review stale.
- The Tesla producer now blocks a new render without that layer; the already-delivered film and its frozen Colab v1 package are preserved.

## Revision state

The new eight-rung draft passes structural checks. Its estimated 209.3-second runtime is provisional; speech has not been generated for the full draft. Measured narration, actual visual execution and an editorial audition are still required before a new full render. The visual-direction sample tests only one micro hook and payoff.

No viewer-retention improvement has been measured. Verification after publishing requires the actual YouTube retention curve and attention to specific drop-offs, not an invented numeric quality score.
