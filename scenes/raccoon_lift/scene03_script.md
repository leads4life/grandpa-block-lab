# Raccoon Lift -- Scene 03: "Up High, But..." (written under the standing authorization)

**Target:** ~60 s, 16:9. Next morning by the stream, then that evening (golden light). Captions as .srt.
**Lesson:** CLIMBERS -- sharp claws, and back feet that turn around so raccoons climb down head first (lesson panel).
**Build:** a tall tower (four posts, a platform 1.5 m up) by the stream, the food box built on top.
**Safety:** ladders only with a grown-up holding the bottom; watch from far away; never feed.

Science check: raccoons are excellent climbers with sharp, non-retractable claws; they can rotate their hind feet ~180
degrees to descend trees head first; they climb posts and drainpipes easily.
